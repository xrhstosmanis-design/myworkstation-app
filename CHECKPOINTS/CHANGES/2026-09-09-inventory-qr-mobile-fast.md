# Inventory 2.0 — QR sharing and mobile fast count

- The owner access-grant action now renders a real QR code for the generated temporary URL.
- The grant card includes the PIN, open-link, copy-link-and-PIN and native share actions.
- The QR/PIN counter screen now follows the same scan → quantity → save-next flow as BackOffice.
- Repeated scans accumulate quantities and the most recently counted product appears first.
- The mobile screen defaults to counted products, with an explicit option to show all stocktake lines.
- Access remains scoped to the selected stocktake/zone, temporary, PIN-protected and limited-use.

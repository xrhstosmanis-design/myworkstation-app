# No 5 · Employee contact and availability

The opt-in employees.edit grant requires employees.read and enables a scoped editor on the existing workforce employee read panel. It updates phone, email and four availability fields for active employees based only at the selected store. The transaction rechecks the live grant, exact company/store, revision and assignment, and records an audit with the reason. Existing Owner employee flows, PINs, wages, roles and account access stay on their original paths.
